Squirrel Studies

Five takes on one character, each drawn in the same five poses. Every shape is a smooth spline: there are no straight lines, only curves that meet in soft points at the nose, toes, elbows, knees and fur tips. The figure is a flat dark-grey silhouette; thin white lines show where limbs, head and tail overlap, and the white of the eyes carries the whole expression. Rows are the five versions; columns are the five pose-and-expression pairs. The poses are shared joint positions, so differences between rows come only from each version's proportions and curve style.
